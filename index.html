About the Foundation

Creating Opportunity.
Building Momentum.

We believe support should not simply help people survive — it should help people move forward. That means meeting immediate needs today while opening doors to lasting independence tomorrow.

Immediate support — essential help for people and families when they need it most.
Education & mentorship — practical knowledge that builds confidence and skills.
Entrepreneurial opportunity — pathways to new income and ownership.
Veteran support & community programs — standing with those who served and the neighbors around us.
Long-term empowerment — helping people become self-sufficient, not dependent on temporary aid.

Our Mission

Everyone deserves a real path forward.

The Momintum Foundation is committed to helping individuals and families overcome difficult circumstances while creating opportunities for long-term independence. We believe everyone deserves access to food, education, opportunity, dignity, and the resources needed to build a better future.

For Our Veterans

Serving Those Who Served Us

Veterans can face major challenges when transitioning into civilian life — and those challenges are often greater for veterans living with disabilities.

The Momintum Foundation wants to help veterans discover new opportunities, develop entrepreneurial skills, explore alternative income paths, and build sustainable futures — with dignity and respect at every step.
